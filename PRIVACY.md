# Grabit privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Grabit is a Chrome extension that exports AI chat conversations to files. This page explains what it does with your data.

## The short version

Grabit has no server. Your conversations are read and converted inside your own browser, and the exported file is saved to your own computer.

## What Grabit reads

When you click export, Grabit reads the conversation that is open in your current tab on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok. It does not read other tabs, other sites or your browsing history.

## What Grabit stores

Grabit stores a small amount of information in Chrome's extension storage on your device:

- your settings
- the count of free exports used today
- your licence key, if you have entered one

It does not store your conversations.

## When data leaves your browser

Only in these two cases, and only when you use the feature:

1. **Notion sync.** When you sync a conversation to Notion, Grabit sends that conversation to your Notion workspace through the official Notion API, using the access you granted. Notion's own privacy policy applies to data stored there.
2. **Licence check.** When you enter a Pro licence key, Grabit sends the key to LemonSqueezy to confirm it is valid. LemonSqueezy handles purchases and its own privacy policy applies to payment details. Grabit never sees your card details.

## What Grabit does not do

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising
- No user accounts
- No selling or sharing of data

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything Grabit stored in your browser. Files you exported stay on your computer until you delete them. To revoke Notion access, remove the Grabit connection in your Notion settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
